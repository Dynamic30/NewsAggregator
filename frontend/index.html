When several outlets cover one event, their articles become a single card. The badge shows how many outlets covered it.

6 of 6

Open any story

Every outlet's version is listed with a score from 0 to 100. Tap Why? for the reasons, or open the original. Adjust text size and reading theme, or press Listen.

What the score means

A score is an estimate, not a verdict.

Below 50 reads closer to the opposition's side of the story. Above 50 reads closer to the government's. Around 50 is neutral.

  • It is an estimate. We read the article's text and rate its framing. We can be wrong.
  • You can check the work. Every score comes with short reasons, so you can disagree with it.
  • It rates one article. It does not say an outlet is good or bad, or that a story is true or false.
